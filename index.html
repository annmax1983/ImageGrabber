Image Grabber is a bulk image downloader for Chrome and Edge. Open any page and the side panel, and every picture on that page is collected into one place where you can filter by size and type, preview at full size, select what you want and save it all in a single batch. It is for people who collect references, product shots or research material and are tired of right-clicking one image at a time. No account, no ads, no tracking.

01

Everything it does

The core features at a glance.

01

Scan anything: normal images, lazy-loaded sources, the largest candidate in a srcset, CSS background images, video posters and images inside SVG.

02

Original detection: finds the full-size file behind a small preview, marks it HD and falls back to the displayed image when no larger original can be verified.

03

Auto Collect: keeps adding images while the page loads, so nothing is missed on long or infinite-scroll pages.

04

Grid and list views: thumbnails for browsing, a compact table for comparing.

05

Filters: minimum width and height, image type, and sorting by width, height or name.

06

Manual Pick: click the exact element whose image you want, including background images.

Also included

  • Full-size preview with arrow-key navigation, plus download and copy-URL buttons, and copy URLs for everything that passes the filter.
  • Batch download up to 30 images at a time for free, with a success and failure report at the end.
  • Your list and your removed marks survive reloads, so a long collection session is not lost.
  • Single-page apps are handled: real navigations and in-page route changes clear the old list and rescan the new view.
  • Interface in six languages.

02

Free vs Premium

The free tier is a complete tool, not a trial. Premium is optional.

Free

unlimited scanning and browsing, filters, both views, full-size preview, manual pick, copy URLs, original detection, and batch downloads of up to 30 images at a time in the original format.

Premium

no selection cap, so a whole gallery can go in one run; perceptual deduplication that spots the same picture at a different address; conversion to JPG, PNG or WebP while downloading; compression with a quality slider and a maximum width; and a single advanced pipeline that deduplicates, converts and saves. One key activates on two browsers, and removing the licence from one browser frees that slot again.

No licence is needed to scan or download. Every free feature works without an account. Premium asks the licence server for a check about every two hours. It keeps working while you are offline; a failed check never switches the paid features off.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no tracking. Read exactly what is stored and when the extension uses the network.
